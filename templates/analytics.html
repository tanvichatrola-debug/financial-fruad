Fraud Analytics & Behavioral Pattern Analysis

Exploratory data analytics derived from 550,000 audited transactions revealing structural fraud distributions.

Class Imbalance: Legitimate vs. Fraudulent

Baseline ground truth distribution in the Indian banking dataset
Presentation Explanation: Fraudulent transactions represent only 0.886% (4,873 records) out of 550,000 transactions. This extreme 1:112 imbalance demonstrates why standard accuracy is ineffective and why threshold tuning and PR-AUC are vital for banking security.

Fraud Incidence Rate by Transaction Type

Empirical fraud probability across different banking payment instruments
Presentation Explanation: High-value wire transfers (RTGS at 1.85%) and physical instruments (Cheques at 1.18%) exhibit more than double the fraud rate of micro-payment channels like UPI (0.79%), due to higher financial stakes.

Fraud Incidence Rate by Payment Channel

Comparison of digital, assisted, and physical touchpoints
Presentation Explanation: Traditional Branch counters (0.95%) and POS Terminals (0.92%) experience marginally higher fraud vulnerability compared to Mobile App banking (0.87%), demonstrating that branch-based impersonation remains a persistent threat.

Fraud Frequency by Merchant Category

Absolute count of fraudulent occurrences across top merchant segments
Presentation Explanation: Retail Shopping, E-Commerce, Food & Dining, and Peer Transfers lead in absolute fraud volume because they account for the vast majority of consumer transaction attempts.

Fraud Incidence Rate by Indian State

Regional variation in fraudulent transaction ratios across major states
Presentation Explanation: Fraud rates remain relatively uniform across major commercial hubs (Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Delhi, Tamil Nadu) hovering near the national 0.88% baseline, indicating generalized rather than localized attack surfaces.

Fraud Activity Across 24 Transaction Hours

Hourly curve of fraudulent activity from 00:00 to 23:00 hrs
Presentation Explanation: Fraudulent activity occurs around the clock with notable nocturnal activity between midnight and 05:00 hrs, when automated bots and unauthorized account takeovers capitalize on delayed customer alerts.

Fraud Concentration by Transaction Amount Range

Fraud likelihood segmented by monetary size tiers
Presentation Explanation: The average fraudulent transaction is INR 119,446 compared to INR 29,106 for legitimate transactions. High-value tiers (over INR 100K and over INR 500K) exhibit heightened financial risk exposure, confirming transaction amount as the #1 predictive feature in our model.
